:fouR about us

We are a collective that funds art in all its forms, mainly dance, theatre and music.

Where

Founded in the UK, we’re now working internationally. In 2023 and 2024 we worked with dancers in Vekha on the commercial scene. Now we’re moving into freestyle.

Our question

Which form of dance creates the strongest sense of togetherness?

The research aims to know whether it’s freestyle, commercial, classes or choreography, and which one makes people want to belong to a group.

The funding

All our funding goes into creating these moments and testing them.

Until

2028

The project runs up until 2028, when we are hoping to have our answer.
